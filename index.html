About Us

Shaping Tomorrow's Leaders

Gyan Jyoti Gurukulam Public School, Jandaha (Vaishali), Bihar, is a distinguished educational institution dedicated to providing quality education with a perfect blend of academics, morals, and extracurricular activities.

20+ Years of
Excellence

Our Mission

To impart holistic education that develops intellectual, physical, emotional, and social potential of every student, preparing them to become responsible global citizens.

Our Vision

To be a center of educational excellence that inspires creativity, critical thinking, and lifelong learning in every student who walks through our doors.

Our Values

Integrity, discipline, respect, and compassion form the cornerstone of our educational philosophy, guiding students toward becoming ethical and empathetic individuals.

Our Academic Programs

We offer comprehensive education from Nursery to Secondary, following the CBSE curriculum with focus on conceptual learning and practical application.

Pre-Primary

Nurturing young minds through play-based learning, building foundation for future academic success.

Nursery - KG
  • Activity-based learning
  • Creative arts & music
  • Personality development

Primary

Building strong academic foundations with a balanced curriculum of languages, mathematics, and sciences.

Class I - V
  • CBSE curriculum
  • Smart classrooms
  • Co-curricular focus

Middle School

Deepening knowledge with advanced subjects, practical experiments, and competitive exam preparation.

Class VI - VIII
  • Science laboratories
  • Computer education
  • Olympiad training

Secondary

Preparing students for board examinations with intensive coaching and career guidance programs.

Class IX - X
  • Board exam preparation
  • Career counseling
  • Competitive exams
